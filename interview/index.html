Practice

Interview questions

Questions functional consultants, analysts and engineers are asked, with the shape of a strong answer.

Procure-to-pay

Functional Explain 2-way, 3-way and 4-way matching and when you would use each.

2-way compares invoice and PO (services, low value); 3-way adds the goods receipt (stock goods); 4-way adds inspection acceptance (regulated or quality-critical). Tolerances decide holds.

Functional What is GR/IR and how do you clean it up?

A clearing account between goods receipt and invoice. Ageing balances mean missing invoices or wrong receipts; investigate with suppliers and receivers monthly before adjusting.

Functional How is a service entry sheet different from a goods receipt?

It confirms services performed (period, hours, milestones) and acts as the receipt for matching service invoices.

Technical How would you make invoice ingestion idempotent?

Hash the file and use supplier + invoice number (+ fiscal year) as the business key; store processing state; make posting APIs idempotent on that key.

Technical Design re-matching of held invoices.

Subscribe the matcher to goods-received, PO-changed and inspection-completed events; re-evaluate held invoices for that PO line; store every attempt for audit.

Order management

Functional What is ATP and why use safety stock in stores?

On hand − reserved − safety stock; store counts are less reliable, so safety stock prevents promising phantom units.

Technical How do you prevent overselling the last unit?

Atomic reservation in a transaction with row locks in a fixed order (or conditional updates), a constraint reserved ≤ on hand, and events through an outbox.

Warehouse

Functional Compare wave and waveless picking.

Waves release batches at set times; waveless (order streaming) releases continuously by priority and cut-off. Many sites use both.

Technical How would you integrate a WMS with an ERP that can be offline?

Store-and-forward queues at the site, idempotent confirmations, and reconciliation reports for receipts and shipments.

Planning

Functional How do you compute a reorder point?

Daily demand × lead time + safety stock; safety stock = z × σ(daily demand) × √lead time.

Functional What is the bullwhip effect and how do you reduce it?

Demand variability grows upstream; share POS data, shorten lead times, stabilise prices and order more frequently in smaller lots.

Tax

Functional Supplier in Karnataka, buyer in Tamil Nadu, goods delivered to Tamil Nadu: which GST?

Inter-state → IGST at the full rate; place of supply Tamil Nadu; buyer claims IGST credit.

Functional Why are supplies to an SEZ zero-rated even within the same state?

IGST Act s.7(5)(b) treats them as inter-state, and s.16 zero-rates them: under LUT without IGST or with IGST and refund.

Technical How do you make a tax engine audit-ready?

Deterministic, versioned rules and rates with effective dates, an immutable determination log of inputs and outputs, maker-checker for changes, and reconciliation to returns and e-invoice portals.

Functional Difference between VAT and US sales tax?

VAT is collected at every stage with input credit; US sales tax is collected once at the retail sale, with resale exemptions and use tax when not charged.

Integration

Technical How do you design Kafka topics for inventory events?

Key by sku@node to keep order per row, compacted topics for latest state, schema registry with compatibility rules, and an outbox for reliable publishing.

Technical What is an anti-corruption layer and why use it with ERPs?

A translation layer between your canonical model and vendor-specific objects, so ERP vocabulary does not leak into your core.

Functional What are EDI 850, 855, 856 and 810?

Purchase order, PO acknowledgement, advance ship notice, invoice.

IDP / AI

Technical How do you decide when an extracted invoice needs human review?

Field confidence below a threshold that independent rules do not confirm, or a failed business rule or risk signal; measure touchless rate per supplier.

Technical How do you defend an LLM document pipeline against prompt injection?

Treat document text as data, constrain outputs to a schema, validate against rules, never give the model write tools without approval, and log everything.

Architecture

Technical Where should the PO system of record live when you have Coupa and SAP?

Pick one owner (often S2P for indirect, ERP for direct); the other subscribes. Never generate PO numbers in two places.

Technical How would you make warehouse operations resilient to cloud outages?

Local execution with buffered integration, idempotent sync on recovery, and degraded modes such as paper pick lists for critical flows.

Finance

Functional What is the cash-to-cash cycle?

Days inventory outstanding + days sales outstanding − days payable outstanding.

Functional What is the MSME 45-day rule in India?

Payments to micro and small enterprises must be made within the agreed term, capped at 45 days (15 without agreement); otherwise the deduction moves to the payment year and interest applies.
