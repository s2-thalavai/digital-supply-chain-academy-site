Contract lifecycle management

Beginner lens. A contract only saves money if orders use it.

Contract lifecycle management (CLM) covers a contract from request and drafting through negotiation, approval, signature, execution, obligation tracking, amendments and renewal or expiry. In procurement its main job is making sure POs actually use the negotiated prices and terms.

In plain words

You negotiated a great price for a year. If nobody tells the buyers, they keep buying at the old price, and the saving disappears. CLM keeps the agreement findable, reminds you before it expires and checks every order against it.

Architecture

The contract is the source of truth for price and terms; ERP and catalogs hold copies. Sync on activation and amendment, and reference the contract id on every PO line.

  • Contract id on PO lines enables compliance analytics
  • Effective-dated prices; never overwrite history
  • Obligations as structured data, not only PDF text
  • AI clause extraction helps migrate legacy contracts but needs review

What can go wrong

Failure scenarioHow to handle it
Price leakagePOs at prices above contract. Fix by forcing catalog/contract price lookup and alerting on variances.
Silent auto-renewalUnfavourable contract renews because no one saw the notice date.
Commitment shortfallBuying less than committed volume triggers penalties.
