B2B EDI orders, marketplaces and digital channels

Beginner lens. Many channels, one order system.

Large customers send orders by EDI, retailers sell on marketplaces, and distributors use B2B portals. Each channel brings orders, inventory feeds, shipment notices and invoices in its own format; an integration layer normalises them into one order model.

In plain words

A big retailer sends orders as machine messages, an online marketplace sends them through its API, and a dealer types them into your portal. They should all arrive in your order system looking the same.

Architecture

Never let partner formats leak into the core. Translate at the edge into a canonical model, and keep the original message for audit.

  • Idempotency on partner + external order number
  • Chargebacks for late or incorrect ASNs: monitor compliance
  • Inventory feeds must respect safety stock per channel
