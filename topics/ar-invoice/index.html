Customer invoice, accounts receivable and cash application

Beginner lens. AR = who owes us money and for how long.

After goods ship or services are delivered, billing creates a customer invoice. Accounts receivable (AR) tracks what customers owe, collects it, and cash application matches incoming payments to open invoices. Short payments and deductions become disputes to resolve.

In plain words

You sold something, so you send a bill. AR is the list of everyone who still owes you. When money lands in the bank, cash application works out which bill it pays.

Architecture

AR mirrors AP with different risks: AP risks paying wrongly, AR risks not getting paid. Cash application is a matching problem much like invoice matching.

  • Carry the order/quote reference to the invoice so remittances match
  • Model deductions as separate open items with reasons
  • Reconcile AR sub-ledger to GL daily
