Supplier invoices and accounts payable

Beginner lens. Click each lifecycle state to see what happened to the invoice.

A supplier invoice is a request for payment from a supplier. Accounts payable (AP) captures it, validates it, matches it against the PO and receipt, gets approval where needed, posts it to the ledger and schedules payment. Customer invoices (accounts receivable) are the mirror image and have their own module.

In plain words

Supplier invoice = "Someone is asking our company to pay them." Customer invoice = "Our company is asking a customer to pay us." AP checks the first kind very carefully, because paying the wrong amount, or paying twice, is real money gone.

Architecture

Invoice processing is a pipeline with a human queue at its heart. Design for the exception path, because that is where cost and delay accumulate.

  • Idempotency key: supplier + invoice number + fiscal year
  • Holds are first-class state with reasons and owners
  • Payment is a separate domain (treasury) fed by posted invoices
  • Keep the original image linked to the posted document for audit

What can go wrong

Failure scenarioHow to handle it
Duplicate paymentSame invoice via email and portal. Check supplier + number + amount + date fuzzily, not just exact number.
Bank details changed by emailClassic fraud. Route bank changes through verified supplier master workflows, never from an invoice.
Missed early-payment discountApproval sat for 12 days on a 2/10 NET30 invoice.
