Intercompany flows, stock transfers and transfer pricing

Beginner lens. Moving stock between your own sites still has rules.

Large groups move goods and services between their own sites and legal entities: stock transfers between plants, intercompany sales between companies, and shared services. Each flow needs the right documents, tax treatment (in India, transfers between GSTINs are taxable "supplies between distinct persons"), transfer pricing and elimination at consolidation.

In plain words

Your Bengaluru warehouse sends stock to your Chennai store. No customer is involved, but because the two sites have different GST registrations, the tax authority treats it like a sale. And if two of your companies trade with each other, the price must be fair, as if they were strangers.

Architecture

Treat intercompany as two mirrored documents that must always agree. Automate the mirror posting and reconcile daily.

  • Transfer price from a governed price list
  • Distinct-person GST: valuation under CGST Rule 28 (open market value; the invoice value is deemed open market value when the recipient can claim full ITC)
  • Netting reduces cash movements between entities
  • Consolidation eliminates intercompany revenue, cost and unrealised profit in stock
