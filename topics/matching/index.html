2-way, 3-way and 4-way matching

Matching compares the supplier invoice against other documents before paying. 2-way: invoice vs PO. 3-way: invoice vs PO vs goods receipt. 4-way adds inspection or acceptance. Tolerances decide how much difference is acceptable before the invoice goes on hold.

In plain words

Before paying a bill you check: did I order this (PO)? Did it actually arrive (receipt)? Was it good enough to keep (inspection)? If the bill says 100 but only 95 arrived, you should not pay for 100.

Architecture

Matching is event-driven in practice: an invoice on hold should release itself when the missing receipt or price change arrives.

  • Subscribe the matcher to GoodsReceived and PO price change events
  • Store tolerances as configuration by supplier, category or company
  • Record every match attempt for audit

What can go wrong

Failure scenarioHow to handle it
Partial delivery, full invoiceHold until remaining goods are received or supplier credits.
Price increase not on POPrice hold; buyer updates PO or rejects.
Tolerance set too wideSystematic overbilling slips through. Review variance reports.
