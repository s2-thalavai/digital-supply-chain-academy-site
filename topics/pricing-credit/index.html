Pricing, promotions, credit management and backorders

Beginner lens. The price is built in steps; credit decides whether to ship.

On the sell side, a pricing engine turns list prices, customer agreements, promotions and discounts into the price on the order. Credit management checks that the customer can pay before the order ships. When stock is short, backorder processing and allocation decide who gets what.

In plain words

The shirt costs ₹1,000, but this customer has a 10% contract discount, there is a festival offer, and they still owe you money. The system works out the price, checks their credit, and if stock is short, decides whose order waits.

Architecture

Pricing must be fast and explainable. Return a trace of every condition applied, and snapshot it on the order.

  • Cache price lists; compute promotions at runtime
  • Credit exposure = open AR + open orders + this order
  • Allocation rules are policy data (priority customers, fair share)

What can go wrong

Failure scenarioHow to handle it
Stacked promotionsDiscounts combine beyond intent; define exclusivity rules.
Credit hold after pickingCheck credit before release to warehouse.
Backorders never reviewedStale promises; re-run backorder processing daily.
