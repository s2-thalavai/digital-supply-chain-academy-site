Aftermarket: service parts, field service, warranty and repair

Beginner lens. After the sale: parts, repairs and warranty.

After the sale, customers need spare parts, repairs and maintenance. Aftermarket supply chains plan intermittent spare-parts demand, dispatch technicians, handle warranty claims (and recover costs from suppliers), and run repair and refurbishment loops.

In plain words

Your air conditioner breaks. A technician visits with the right part, fixes it under warranty, and the manufacturer later claims part of the cost from the compressor supplier.

Architecture

Aftermarket planning differs from finished-goods planning: demand is lumpy, criticality matters more than volume, and stock sits close to customers.

  • Croston-type or bootstrapping forecasts for intermittent demand
  • Stock by criticality and SLA, not only ABC
  • Close the loop: failure data to engineering and supplier quality
