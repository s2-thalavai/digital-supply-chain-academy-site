India compliance: GST, e-invoicing, e-way bill, TDS and MSME payments

Beginner lens. Tax credit and supplier payments have rules; software checks them every month.

Indian procure-to-pay must handle GST input tax credit (ITC), e-invoicing with an IRN, e-way bills for moving goods, TDS on purchases and services, and the MSME rule that payments to micro and small enterprises are due within 45 days.

In plain words

You can reclaim the GST you paid suppliers only if their invoice shows up in your GST statement (GSTR-2B) and you pay them within 180 days. Big sellers must register invoices with a government portal. Trucks need an e-way bill. And small suppliers must be paid within 45 days or you lose the tax deduction for that expense until you pay.

Architecture

Treat tax rules as configuration that changes often. Keep supplier tax and MSME attributes in master data, and reconcile ITC monthly against GSTR-2B before claiming.

  • Validate IRN and QR signature on inbound e-invoices
  • Hold ITC for invoices not in GSTR-2B; follow up with supplier
  • Track 180-day payment condition for ITC
  • Section numbers refer to the Income-tax Act, 1961; the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 April 2026 with renumbered sections, so verify current references

Failure scenarioHow to handle it
