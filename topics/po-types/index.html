PO types and services procurement

Beginner lens. Different buying situations need different PO types.

Not every purchase is a one-off order. Companies use standard POs, blanket or framework agreements, scheduling agreements for repeat deliveries, consignment, subcontracting and service orders. Services need different controls: a statement of work, milestones or timesheets, and a service entry instead of a goods receipt.

In plain words

Buying a laptop is one order. Buying printer paper every week is easier with one yearly agreement and small call-offs. Paying a consultant needs proof the work was done, so they submit approved timesheets instead of a delivery note.

Architecture

Model the agreement and each release as separate documents. Matching must know which receipt type to use: goods receipt, service entry, or consignment withdrawal.

  • Consignment liability arises at withdrawal, not at receipt
  • Subcontracting needs component stock "at supplier"
  • Services: rate cards and SOW deliverables as structured data

What can go wrong

Failure scenarioHow to handle it
Blanket PO overspentNo cap enforcement; check consumption on each call-off.
Services paid without proofNo service entry step; require approval before matching.
Consignment stock not settledSupplier unpaid for withdrawals; run periodic settlement.
