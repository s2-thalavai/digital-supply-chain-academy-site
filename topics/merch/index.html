Retail merchandising: assortment, allocation, replenishment and markdowns

Beginner lens. What to sell where, and when to cut the price.

Merchandising decides what to sell, where and at what price: assortment planning picks the range per store cluster, allocation splits initial buys across stores, replenishment keeps them stocked, and markdown optimisation clears seasonal stock at the best margin.

In plain words

Winter jackets: which styles in which stores, how many each store gets at launch, refilling the best sellers, and when to cut prices so the last ones sell before spring.

Architecture

Merchandising is planning-heavy and seasonal. Price changes must reach POS, e-commerce and shelf labels consistently.

  • Size curves per store cluster
  • Markdown cadence aligned to label printing / ESLs
  • Feedback from returns into assortment

What can go wrong

Failure scenarioHow to handle it
Late markdownsDeep clearance at season end destroys margin.
Wrong size mixStores run out of M while L sits.
Price mismatchShelf vs POS vs online differ.
