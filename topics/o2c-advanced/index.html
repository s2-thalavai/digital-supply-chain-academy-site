Collections, dunning, revenue recognition and subscription billing

Beginner lens. Remind late payers; book revenue when it is earned.

Collections turn receivables into cash: aging, dunning reminders, worklists and dispute handling. Revenue recognition decides when sales can be booked as revenue (Ind AS 115 / IFRS 15). Subscription billing handles recurring charges, usage and proration.

In plain words

Some customers pay late, so you remind them politely, then firmly. Accountants also need to know when you have earned the money: when you shipped, or over the year of a subscription.

Architecture

Collections is a prioritisation problem; revenue recognition is a rules-and-schedules problem. Keep billing events and revenue schedules linked to the same contract.

  • Dunning levels configurable by customer segment
  • Disputes pause dunning for the disputed amount
  • Revenue schedules generated from performance obligations

What can go wrong

Failure scenarioHow to handle it
Dunning a disputed invoiceAnnoys good customers; pause on dispute.
Revenue recognised at billing for a year-long serviceShould be spread over the service period.
No promise-to-pay trackingCollectors call the same customer twice.
