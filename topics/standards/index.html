GS1 identifiers and EDI standards

Beginner lens. Shared numbers and shared message formats.

Supply chains share data through standards. GS1 identifies things: GTIN for products, GLN for locations, SSCC for pallets and cartons, with GS1-128 and 2D barcodes carrying them. EDI standards (ANSI X12 in North America, UN/EDIFACT internationally) define the business messages: orders, ship notices, invoices.

In plain words

A barcode number that every company understands, and order and invoice messages that every computer understands, so partners do not have to agree a new format each time.

Architecture

Translate standards at the edge into your canonical model. Keep identifiers as strings (leading zeros matter) and validate check digits.

  • GTIN-14 stored as 14 digits, left-padded with zeros
  • Validate check digits on input
  • Map X12 and EDIFACT to the same canonical documents
