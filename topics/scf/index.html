Working capital and supply chain finance

Beginner lens. Early payment for a discount, or a bank paying early on your behalf.

Supply chain finance (SCF) improves cash flow for buyers and suppliers. With dynamic discounting the buyer pays early in exchange for a discount; with reverse factoring a bank pays the supplier early based on the buyer's approved invoice, and the buyer pays the bank at the due date.

In plain words

A small supplier needs cash now, but you pay in 60 days. Either you pay early and take a small discount, or a bank pays them early (cheaply, because it trusts you) and you pay the bank later.

Architecture

Only approved invoices are eligible. The ERP stays the system of record; the SCF platform changes the payment date and amount through an agreed interface.

  • Prevent double payment: original payment must be suppressed
  • Accounting treatment of reverse factoring may require disclosure; involve finance
  • Treat funder data as external and reconcile daily

What can go wrong

Failure scenarioHow to handle it
Double paymentBuyer pays supplier at due date after the funder already did.
Unattractive offersAPR too low for suppliers or too high for buyer; calibrate.
Disclosure issuesReverse factoring presented as payables without review.
