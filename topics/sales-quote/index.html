Sales quotation

A sales quotation is the seller's offer to a customer: products, quantities, prices, discounts, taxes, freight, and how long the offer is valid. When the customer accepts, the quote converts into a sales order. It is the mirror image of a procurement RFQ.

In plain words

Procurement RFQ: the buyer asks suppliers "what would you charge?" Sales quotation: the seller tells a customer "this is what we will charge." Same conversation, opposite side of the table.

Architecture

The quote belongs to CRM/CPQ; the order belongs to OMS/ERP. The conversion is a hand-off with a clear owner and an idempotency key (quote id + revision).

  • Re-price or re-validate at conversion if the quote is near expiry
  • Snapshot prices and taxes on the quote so acceptance honours what the customer saw
  • Check availability at quote time for promise dates (ATP quote)

What can go wrong

Failure scenarioHow to handle it
Accepted after expiryValidity date enforced at conversion; re-quote.
Discount approved by nobodyApproval rules missing for a new product line.
Quote promises stock that is goneATP check at quote time without reservation; re-check at order.
