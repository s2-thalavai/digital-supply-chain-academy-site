Logistics & Transportation

Moving goods between nodes: choosing modes and carriers, agreeing who pays and who carries the risk, and the documents that travel with the freight.

Freight and carrier management

Buying, planning and controlling freight so goods move reliably at the right cost.

  • Shipment types: parcel, LTL (less than truckload), FTL (full truckload), FCL/LCL containers
  • Carrier selection and tendering; contract vs spot rates
  • Load building and consolidation to cut empty space
  • Tracking and exception management
  • Freight audit and pay: check invoices against rates and proof of delivery
  • Carrier scorecards: on-time %, damage, tender acceptance

Incoterms and shipping documentation

Incoterms 2020 are the ICC rules that define where risk passes from seller to buyer, and who pays for carriage, insurance and customs.

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of lading (ocean) or air waybill (air): receipt, contract of carriage, and for ocean often title to the goods
  • Certificate of origin: decides duty rates under trade agreements
  • Customs declarations: in India, the shipping bill on export and bill of entry on import
  • E-way bill in India for moving goods above the threshold set by GST rules
  • Proof of delivery (POD)

Top 20 logistics terms

3PL
Third-party logistics provider running warehousing or transport for you.
ASN
Advance shipping notice: what is on the way, before it arrives.
Bill of lading
Carrier's receipt and contract of carriage for freight.
Consolidation
Combining small shipments into one larger load.
Cross-docking
Unloading inbound goods straight onto outbound trucks with little or no storage.
Demurrage
Charge for a container left at the port or terminal beyond the free time.
Detention
Charge for keeping a carrier's container or trailer outside the terminal too long.
Drayage
Short-haul trucking of containers to and from ports or rail yards.
Freight forwarder
Arranges transport, documents and customs on the shipper's behalf.
FTL / LTL
Full truckload vs less-than-truckload shipments.
Incoterms
ICC rules on risk, cost and responsibilities between seller and buyer.
Last mile
Final delivery leg to the customer; often the most expensive.
Lead time
Time from order to receipt.
Manifest
List of all shipments on a vehicle or vessel.
Pallet
Platform that lets goods be moved by forklift as one unit.
POD
Proof of delivery, signed or electronic.
Reverse logistics
Moving goods back from customers: returns, repairs, recycling.
SKU
Stock-keeping unit: one specific sellable item variant.
TEU
Twenty-foot equivalent unit, the standard container size measure.
Transit time
Time goods spend moving between origin and destination.
