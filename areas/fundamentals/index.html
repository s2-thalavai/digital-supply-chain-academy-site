Supply Chain Fundamentals

What a supply chain is, the parts it is made of, and the questions every newcomer asks.

What is a supply chain?

The network of people, processes, technology and resources involved in creating a product and delivering it from raw materials to the end customer.

  • A supply chain is the network; supply chain management is how you run it
  • Every supply chain carries three flows: goods, information and money
  • Even a service business has one: software needs data centres, consultants need laptops

Top 20 questions about supply chain

What is a supply chain?

The network of organisations, people, activities, information and resources that make a product and get it to the customer.

What is supply chain management?

Planning and coordinating that network so goods, information and money flow efficiently from suppliers to customers.

What is the difference between logistics and supply chain management?

Logistics moves and stores goods. SCM is broader: it also covers planning, sourcing, production and supplier relationships.

What is the difference between procurement and purchasing?

Purchasing is the transactional act of ordering and paying. Procurement also includes sourcing, negotiation, contracts and supplier management.

What is the SCOR model?

A reference framework from ASCM. The classic version has Plan, Source, Make, Deliver, Return (plus Enable); the SCOR Digital Standard uses Orchestrate, Plan, Order, Source, Transform, Fulfill, Return.

What is the bullwhip effect?

Demand variability grows as you move upstream, because each tier reacts to orders rather than real consumer demand.

What is lead time?

The time between placing an order and receiving it.

What is safety stock?

Extra inventory held to protect against uncertainty in demand or supply.

What does OTIF mean?

On time, in full: the share of orders delivered complete by the promised date.

What is a 3PL?

A third-party logistics provider that runs warehousing, transport or both for you.

What is just-in-time (JIT)?

Receiving materials only as they are needed, to cut inventory. It needs reliable suppliers.

What is an ERP?

Enterprise resource planning: the integrated system of record for finance, purchasing, inventory and sales.

What do a WMS and a TMS do?

A WMS runs the inside of a warehouse; a TMS plans and executes freight transport.

What is S&OP?

A monthly process that aligns demand, supply and financial plans into one agreed plan.

What are Incoterms?

ICC rules defining where risk passes and who pays for transport, insurance and customs in a trade.

What is reverse logistics?

Moving goods back from customers for return, repair, refurbishment, recycling or disposal.

What is a lean supply chain?

One designed to remove waste and deliver steady flow at low cost, best for stable demand.

What is an agile supply chain?

One designed to respond quickly to volatile demand, using flexible capacity and postponement.

What is the cash-to-cash cycle?

Days inventory + days receivable − days payable: how long cash is tied up in operations.

What skills does a supply chain career need?

Process thinking, data analysis, negotiation, communication, systems knowledge (ERP/WMS/TMS) and problem solving.
