Forecasting methods

Qualitative methods use judgement; quantitative methods use data. Most companies combine both.

QualitativeQuantitative
Delphi method (expert rounds)Moving average
Market research and surveysExponential smoothing, Holt-Winters
Sales force compositeRegression with causal drivers (price, promotion, weather)
Executive opinionMachine learning (gradient boosting, neural nets)
Best for new products, no historyBest for items with stable history

Forecast accuracy (MAPE, MAD)

Measure error to improve the forecast and to size safety stock.

MeasureFormulaBasis
MADaverage of |actual − forecast|units
MAPEaverage of |actual − forecast| ÷ actual × 100% (unstable when actuals are near zero)
WMAPEΣ|actual − forecast| ÷ Σ actual% weighted by volume
BiasΣ(forecast − actual) ÷ Σ actual+ over-forecast, − under-forecast
Tracking signalΣ(actual − forecast) ÷ MADbeyond ±4 suggests a biased model

Sales and operations planning (S&OP)

A monthly cycle that agrees one plan across sales, operations and finance.

  1. Data gathering. Update history, open orders, inventory.
  2. Demand review. Sales and marketing agree the unconstrained demand plan.
  3. Supply review. Operations checks capacity, materials and inventory.
  4. Pre-S&OP reconciliation. Resolve gaps; prepare scenarios and financial impact.
  5. Executive S&OP. Leaders decide and approve one plan.
