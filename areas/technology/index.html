ERP systems (SAP, Oracle and others)

ERP is the system of record for finance, purchasing, inventory and sales. Specialist systems (S2P, OMS, WMS, TMS, planning) connect around it.

Technology landscapeCapability matrixSAP learning pathOracle learning pathVendor comparison

AI agents innovating supply chain

Software agents that watch data, explain exceptions, recommend actions and, within limits you set, act: re-routing an order, releasing a held invoice, raising a purchase order.

  • Start read-only: answer "why is this order late?"
  • Add actions behind human approval
  • Log every tool call; never let documents act as instructions
p-copilotAI automation lab

Intelligent document processing (IDP)

Supply chains run on documents: invoices, order confirmations, bills of lading, packing lists, certificates of analysis, customs entries, delivery receipts and contracts. IDP reads them into validated data with OCR, classification, extraction, confidence scoring, business rules and human review; GenAI adds template-free extraction, summaries and Q&A.

  • Start with high-volume documents (invoices, delivery notes), then the long tail
  • Route only low-confidence fields and failed rules to people
  • Measure straight-through rate, field accuracy and cost per page

Autonomous supply chain

Planning and execution that sense, decide and act with little human intervention. Most companies move through levels: manual → assisted → partly automated → highly automated → autonomous for routine decisions, with people managing exceptions and policy.

Emerging technologies

AI and machine learning
Forecasting, anomaly detection, document extraction, routing.
Generative AI
Drafting, summarising, copilots over operational data.
IoT sensors
Location, temperature and shock tracking, especially for the cold chain.
RFID
Item-level identification without line-of-sight scanning.
Robotics and AMRs
Picking, moving and sorting in warehouses.
Drones
Inventory counting in high racks; niche deliveries.
Blockchain
Shared ledgers for traceability; results are mixed outside specific use cases.
Digital twins
Virtual model of the network for what-if simulation.
Control towers
Central visibility and decision hub.
