Four levels, each answering a harder question.

SCM control tower

A central hub that combines data from ERP, OMS, WMS, TMS and partners to give end-to-end visibility, raise alerts, support decisions and coordinate responses.

  • Visibility: one view of orders, inventory and shipments
  • Alerts: exceptions before they become customer problems
  • Decision support: scenarios and recommended actions
  • Orchestration: trigger workflows across systems
System mapp-twinp-ref-arch

Transparency and visibility

Visibility is knowing where your goods, orders and inventory are right now. Transparency is sharing how and where products are made with customers, investors and regulators.

  • Visibility needs events: ASNs, scans, tracking, inventory changes
  • Transparency needs traceability and trustworthy supplier data
