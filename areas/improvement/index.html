Continuous Improvement: Lean, Kaizen, Six Sigma

Methods for removing waste and variation, step by step, with the people who do the work.

Lean principles and the 8 wastes

Lean maximises customer value while removing waste. Five principles: define value, map the value stream, create flow, establish pull, pursue perfection.

Waste (DOWNTIME)Supply chain example
DefectsMispicks, damaged goods, invoice errors
OverproductionBuilding stock nobody ordered
WaitingTrucks queuing at the dock; invoices waiting for approval
Non-utilised talentIgnoring ideas from pickers and drivers
TransportationUnnecessary moves between sites
InventoryExcess stock hiding problems
MotionPickers walking to far-away locations
Extra processingRe-keying data, unnecessary approvals

Kaizen

"Change for the better": continuous, small improvements by everyone, every day, plus focused kaizen events of a few days on one process.

  • Go to the gemba (where the work happens)
  • PDCA cycle: plan, do, check, act
  • Standardise the improvement, then improve again

Lean Six Sigma

Six Sigma reduces variation using data; lean removes waste. Together they improve speed and quality.

  • Six Sigma quality: 3.4 defects per million opportunities
  • Belts: Yellow, Green, Black, Master Black
  1. Define. Problem, customer, scope, goal.
  2. Measure. Baseline the process with data.
  3. Analyze. Find root causes.
  4. Improve. Test and implement solutions.
  5. Control. Sustain gains with standards and monitoring.

Continuous improvement tools

5S
Sort, set in order, shine, standardise, sustain.
Value stream mapping
Draw material and information flow to expose waste.
5 Whys
Ask why repeatedly to reach the root cause.
Fishbone (Ishikawa)
Group causes into categories (people, method, machine, material, measurement, environment).
Pareto chart
Focus on the few causes that create most problems (80/20).
Kanban
Visual signals that pull replenishment.
Poka-yoke
Mistake-proofing, such as scan-to-confirm.
SMED
Single-minute exchange of dies: cut changeover time.
Gemba walk
Leaders observe the actual work.
A3 report
One-page problem solving story.
