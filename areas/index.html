12 knowledge areas

Everything supply chain management covers, grouped the way practitioners and certification bodies teach it. Each area links to the process deep dives that go further.

1

Supply Chain Fundamentals

What a supply chain is, the parts it is made of, and the questions every newcomer asks.

2

Supplier Management & Procurement

How organisations decide what to buy, from whom, at what price and on what terms, and how they keep suppliers performing.

3

Logistics & Transportation

Moving goods between nodes: choosing modes and carriers, agreeing who pays and who carries the risk, and the documents that travel with the freight.

4

Inventory Management

Holding the right stock in the right place: classifying it, costing it, measuring it and replenishing it.

5

Distribution, Fulfillment & Warehousing

Getting orders out of the building and to the customer: who runs the warehouse, how it is laid out and automated, and what good looks like.

6

Demand Planning & Forecasting

Estimating demand, measuring how wrong the estimate was, and agreeing one plan across the business.

7

Supply Chain Strategy & Design

Structural choices made once and lived with for years: how centralised to be, what to make or buy, when to customise, and which operating model fits the product.

8

Risk Management & Resilience

Identifying what could break the chain, reducing the chance and the damage, and recovering fast when it happens.

9

Continuous Improvement: Lean, Kaizen, Six Sigma

Methods for removing waste and variation, step by step, with the people who do the work.

10

Technology in Supply Chain

The systems that run supply chains today and the technologies changing them.

11

Sustainability & ESG

Measuring and reducing environmental impact, keeping materials in use, and making sure products are made responsibly.

12

Supply Chain Analytics

Turning supply chain data into decisions: what happened, why, what will happen, and what to do.
