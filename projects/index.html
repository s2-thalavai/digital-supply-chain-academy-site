Suggested build order

  1. Foundation: Synthetic supply chain data generator. Every other project uses its data.
  2. Buy side: Procure-to-pay core platform. The backbone for invoices, matching and suppliers.
  3. Buy side: 2/3/4-way matching engine. Pure logic, quick win, strong tests.
  4. Buy side: AP automation platform. Turns invoice-rag into a full AP product.
  5. Sell side: Order orchestration and holds. Completes omni-retail's order lifecycle.
  6. Sell side: Store fulfilment app (BOPIS and ship-from-store). Makes omnichannel tangible.
  7. Execution: Mini WMS. Closes the loop between orders and stock.
  8. Planning: Demand forecasting service driving reorder points. Makes replenishment smart.
  9. Capstone: Reference architecture: the connected platform. Everything running together, observable, on any Kubernetes.
