Settle

Close the money loop: match and pay supplier invoices (end of P2P), bill and collect from customers (end of O2C), and report tax.

0%
0 of 9 done7 lessons · 1 simulation · quiz · about 6.5 h
Next lessonSupplier invoices and accounts payable

Settling turns the physical flow into money. On the buy side, accounts payable captures the supplier invoice, matches it against the PO and goods receipt (three-way match) and pays it in a payment run, which ends procure-to-pay. On the sell side, billing issues the customer invoice, accounts receivable applies incoming payments and chases late ones, which ends order-to-cash. Tax rides on every invoice: in India, CGST and SGST within a state, IGST between states, e-invoices with an IRN and input tax credit. How fast cash goes out and comes in decides the cash conversion cycle.

Key idea 1Three-way match

Pay only what was ordered (PO), received (GRN) and billed (invoice), within tolerance.

Key idea 2Cash is the finish line

O2C ends when the customer's payment is applied to the invoice, not when the parcel is delivered.

Key idea 3Place of supply

Supplier location and place of supply decide CGST + SGST (same state) or IGST (different states).

By the end of this module you can

  • Explain 2-way, 3-way and 4-way matching and tolerances
  • Explain GR/IR and the payment run
  • Apply cash, handle a short payment and calculate DSO
  • Apply the basic GST rule: CGST + SGST within a state, IGST between states
  • Calculate the cash conversion cycle (DIO + DSO − DPO)

Remember these

  1. Pay: invoice → three-way match → payment run → remittance; GR/IR should clear.
  2. Collect: customer invoice → cash application → collections; apply what was paid and track deductions.
  3. DSO = receivables ÷ revenue × days; CCC = DIO + DSO − DPO.
  4. GST: CGST + SGST within a state, IGST between states; e-invoices get an IRN.
  5. Pay MSME (micro and small) suppliers within 45 days in India.

Common mix-ups

✗ Myth: If the invoice matches the PO, it can be paid.

✓ Fact: For stock goods, the receipt must also match (three-way). Otherwise you may pay for goods that never arrived.

✗ Myth: O2C ends when the goods are delivered.

✓ Fact: It ends when the customer's payment is received and applied to the invoice.

✗ Myth: GST type depends on where the buyer's head office is.

✓ Fact: It depends on the supplier's location and the place of supply under the IGST Act rules, which for goods is usually where delivery ends.

✗ Myth: Paying suppliers later is always good.

✓ Fact: It improves DPO but can raise prices, hurt supplier health and, for MSME suppliers in India, break the 45-day rule.

Interview questions

Try answering aloud before opening the model answer.

Explain 2-way, 3-way and 4-way matching.

2-way compares invoice to PO (services, low value). 3-way adds the goods receipt (stock goods). 4-way adds quality inspection acceptance (regulated or quality-critical items). Tolerances decide when a difference blocks payment.

What is GR/IR and why do auditors look at it?

It is the clearing account between goods receipt and invoice receipt. Old open items show goods received but never invoiced, or invoices without receipts, which can mean accrual errors or duplicate payments.

How would you reduce invoice exceptions?

Clean supplier and price master data, catalogue and contract pricing on POs, ASNs and accurate receiving, e-invoicing or IDP capture, sensible tolerances and supplier self-service for disputes.

How would you reduce DSO?

Invoice accurately and immediately on shipment, use e-invoicing, set and enforce credit limits, automate dunning, resolve disputes and deductions quickly, and offer digital payment options.

When is IGST charged instead of CGST and SGST?

When the supplier's location and the place of supply are in different states or union territories, and on imports and supplies to SEZs.

How does inventory affect working capital?

Inventory ties up cash until it is sold and collected. Cutting days of inventory and DSO, or sensibly extending DPO, shortens the cash conversion cycle and frees cash.
