Receive

Expect, check and accept what arrives: the shipping notice, the goods receipt and quality inspection.

0%
0 of 3 done2 lessons · quiz · about 2.5 h
Next lessonReceiving and the goods receipt

Receiving proves the company got what it ordered. The supplier sends an advance shipping notice (ASN) so the warehouse can plan the dock. On arrival, staff scan pallet labels (GS1 SSCC), count and inspect, then post a goods receipt (GR) for what they accept. The receipt increases inventory and creates a liability on the GR/IR clearing account until the invoice arrives. Damaged goods go to blocked stock and back to the supplier; quality-critical items go through inspection first.

Key idea 1ASN first

Knowing what is coming lets the warehouse plan labour and receive by scanning instead of counting.

Key idea 2Receive what you accept

Post only accepted quantity; the invoice will be matched against it.

Key idea 3The receipt is a liability

A goods receipt debits inventory and credits GR/IR until the supplier's invoice clears it.

By the end of this module you can

  • Explain what an ASN is for and how SSCC barcodes speed up receiving
  • Post the right goods receipt when some goods are damaged
  • Explain what a goods receipt does to stock and to finance (GR/IR)
  • Name the GS1 identifiers and EDI messages used at receipt

Remember these

  1. ASN → dock → scan SSCC and count → inspect → goods receipt → putaway.
  2. Post only accepted quantity; return the rest to the supplier.
  3. A goods receipt adds stock and credits GR/IR until the invoice arrives.
  4. GS1: GTIN for products, SSCC for pallets and cartons, GLN for locations.
  5. Services are confirmed with a service entry sheet instead of a goods receipt.

Common mix-ups

✗ Myth: Post the goods receipt for the PO quantity to save time.

✓ Fact: Post what physically arrived and was accepted. A wrong receipt means paying for goods you never got.

Interview questions

Try answering aloud before opening the model answer.

Why is the WMS-to-ERP receipt confirmation so important?

Accounts payable matches invoices against the goods receipt. If the receipt is late or wrong, invoices go on hold or the company pays for goods it did not receive.

What is the difference between a GTIN and an SSCC?

A GTIN identifies a product type (every bottle of the same shampoo). An SSCC identifies one specific logistic unit, such as a single pallet, so it can be tracked through the chain.
