Deliver

Move goods to the customer, across borders if needed, prove delivery and handle returns.

0%
0 of 5 done3 lessons · 1 simulation · quiz · about 3 h
Next lessonTransportation management: planning, tendering, tracking and freight audit

A transportation management system (TMS) plans how goods move: it builds loads, chooses mode and carrier, tenders the shipment, tracks it and audits the freight bill. In India, most movements of goods worth over ₹50,000 need an e-way bill before the truck leaves. Across borders, global trade management handles classification, customs filings, duty and screening, and Incoterms decide who pays for and carries the risk of each leg. Delivery ends with a proof of delivery (POD), which often triggers the customer invoice; returns come back with a return authorisation (RMA).

Key idea 1Consolidate

Combining small shipments into full loads is the biggest freight saving.

Key idea 2Incoterms split cost and risk

They decide where responsibility passes from seller to buyer, not who owns the goods.

Key idea 3POD closes delivery

Signed proof of delivery confirms the customer got the goods and often triggers billing.

By the end of this module you can

  • Describe the TMS cycle: plan, tender, execute, track, settle
  • Compare FTL, LTL, parcel and ocean shipping
  • Read the common Incoterms (EXW, FCA, FOB, CIF, DAP, DDP)
  • Name the transport, customs and delivery documents
  • Explain drop-ship flows, where the supplier delivers directly

Remember these

  1. TMS: plan → tender → pick up → track → deliver → audit and pay the carrier.
  2. Consolidating loads is the main lever on freight cost.
  3. Incoterms split cost and risk; FOB and CIF are sea-only, FCA suits containers.
  4. Documents: bill of lading or air waybill, e-way bill, commercial invoice, certificate of origin, shipping bill or bill of entry, POD.
  5. Returns come back on an RMA; drop-ship lets the supplier deliver straight to your customer.

Common mix-ups

✗ Myth: Incoterms decide when ownership passes.

✓ Fact: Incoterms cover delivery, cost and risk. Ownership (title) passes according to the sales contract.

✗ Myth: Use FOB for every container shipment.

✓ Fact: For containers handed over at a terminal, FCA is usually the correct term under Incoterms 2020.

Interview questions

Try answering aloud before opening the model answer.

How does a TMS reduce freight cost?

Consolidation and load building, mode optimisation (parcel vs LTL vs FTL), competitive tendering against contracted rates, route optimisation and freight audit to stop overbilling.

What documents does an import shipment need?

Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or air waybill, certificate of origin where a trade agreement applies, any licences, and the bill of entry filed with customs.
