Supply chain basics in 4 weeks

Eight modules that follow the supply chain loop, about 1½ hours a day on weekdays. By the end you can follow an order from forecast to cash, name every document on the way, and explain which system handles each step.

A typical week

About 1½ hours a day, 5 days a week

  1. Day 1Module overview and flow
  2. Days 2–4One or two lessons a day; mark each complete
  3. Day 5Practice, quiz and summary

Busy week? Each module stands on its own, so you can move at your own pace. Your progress is saved in this browser.

Weekly plan

  1. Week 1≈ 7.5 h

    Plan and source

    See the big picture, plan demand and stock, and choose suppliers and contracts (source-to-contract).

    • Module 1 · 3.5 h · 3 lessonsPlanSee how a supply chain fits together, then decide how much to buy, make and keep in stock.0%
    • Module 2 · 4 h · 4 lessonsSourceDecide who to buy from and on what terms: spend analysis, RFI/RFQ/RFP, award, contract and supplier onboarding.0%
  2. Week 2≈ 8 h

    Buy, receive and make

    Raise purchase orders, receive and check goods, and turn materials into products.

    • Module 3 · 2.5 h · 2 lessonsBuyTurn a need into an approved, legally binding purchase order, the start of procure-to-pay (P2P).0%
    • Module 4 · 2.5 h · 2 lessonsReceiveExpect, check and accept what arrives: the shipping notice, the goods receipt and quality inspection.0%
    • Module 5 · 3 h · 3 lessonsMakeTurn materials into finished products: bills of materials, MRP, production orders and what the product really cost.0%
  3. Week 3≈ 9 h

    Store, fulfil and deliver

    Run the warehouse, take and promise customer orders, ship them and handle returns.

    • Module 6 · 6 h · 6 lessonsStore & fulfilHold stock accurately, then take, promise and pick customer orders across stores, websites and marketplaces.0%
    • Module 7 · 3 h · 3 lessonsDeliverMove goods to the customer, across borders if needed, prove delivery and handle returns.0%
  4. Week 4≈ 8.5 h

    Settle and the final case

    Match, pay, bill, collect and report tax, then prove it with an end-to-end case.

    • Module 8 · 6.5 h · 7 lessonsSettleClose the money loop: match and pay supplier invoices (end of P2P), bill and collect from customers (end of O2C), and report tax.0%
    • After module 8 · 2 hFinal casePut the eight stages together: follow one product from forecast to cash, play both simulations, then take the final quiz.0%

The four end-to-end processes

Each process runs across several modules: source-to-contract in Source; procure-to-pay across Buy, Receive and Settle; order-to-cash across Store & fulfil, Deliver and Settle.

Optional material (knowledge areas, platform paths, build projects) is linked from each module's Go deeper list, so it never gets in the way. Practice anytime from Practice.
