FreshMart is a grocery retailer with 40 stores and one distribution centre. You will follow a bag of rice from FreshMart's forecast through buying it from the miller, receiving and storing it, selling it online and in stores, and collecting the money. Final quiz

10 questions. Score 80% or more to complete the course. You can retake it as often as you like.

1. FreshMart sells 400 bags of rice a day. The miller's lead time is 3 days and FreshMart keeps 300 bags of safety stock. When should it reorder?
2. The miller delivers 950 bags against a PO for 1,000, and invoices 1,000. What should happen under three-way matching?
3. Which document tells the DC what is arriving before the truck comes?
4. Moving rice from the DC to a store uses…
5. The miller (Karnataka) invoices FreshMart's DC in Karnataka. Which GST applies?
6. An online customer's order is picked from the nearest store. Which OMS capability decided that?
7. FreshMart's DIO is 20 days, DSO is 2 days (card payments) and DPO is 30 days. What is its cash conversion cycle?
8. Which system is FreshMart's system of record for inventory value and supplier payments?
9. Why do sales from the end of the chain matter at the start?
10. From the miller's point of view, FreshMart's purchase order is…

Remember these

  1. Plan → Buy → Receive → Pay → Replenish → Sell → Deliver → Collect → Plan again.
  2. Every hand-off has a document: PO, ASN, GRN, invoice, STO, SO, pick list, POD, customer invoice.
  3. One company's P2P is its supplier's O2C.
  4. ERP holds the money; OMS, WMS and TMS run the work; planning closes the loop.

Common mix-ups

✗ Myth: I finished all the lessons, so I know it.

✓ Fact: Explain the FreshMart flow aloud without notes, naming each document. That is the real test.

Interview questions

Try answering aloud before opening the model answer.

Walk me through a product's journey from forecast to cash.

Forecast and replenishment plan → PR and PO to the supplier → ASN and goods receipt → three-way match and payment → stock transfer to stores → customer order, ATP and sourcing → pick, pack, ship, POD → invoice or card settlement and cash application → sales data feeds the next forecast.
