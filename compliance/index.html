Quality & operations

ISO 9001

Certifiable standard

Quality management system: customer focus, process approach, risk-based thinking, continual improvement.

  • Clauses 4–10: context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement
  • Documented processes and records
  • Internal audits and management review
  • Corrective action
Applies to
Suppliers, manufacturers, 3PLs, software vendors
Assurance
Third-party certification audit; 3-year cycle with surveillance audits

ISO 14001

Certifiable standard

Environmental management system.

  • Environmental aspects and impacts
  • Legal compliance obligations
  • Objectives and operational controls
  • Emergency preparedness
Applies to
Manufacturers, warehouses, logistics
Assurance
Certification audit

ISO 45001

Certifiable standard

Occupational health and safety management.

  • Hazard identification and risk assessment
  • Worker participation
  • Incident investigation
Applies to
Warehouses, plants, fleets
Assurance
Certification audit

ISO 37001

Certifiable standard

Anti-bribery management system.

  • Due diligence on partners
  • Financial and non-financial controls
  • Reporting and investigation
Applies to
Procurement, sales, government contracting
Assurance
Certification audit

ISO 22000 / FSSC 22000 / HACCP

Certifiable standard

Food safety management across the food chain.

  • Hazard analysis and critical control points
  • Traceability and recall
  • Prerequisite programmes
Applies to
Food manufacturers, cold chain, retailers
Assurance
Certification audit

IATF 16949 · AS9100 · ISO 13485

Certifiable standard

Sector quality systems for automotive, aerospace and medical devices.

  • Customer-specific requirements
  • Product safety, traceability, PPAP / FAI
Applies to
Suppliers in those sectors
Assurance
Certification audit

GMP / GDP

Regulatory guideline

Good manufacturing and good distribution practice for pharmaceuticals.

  • Validated processes, batch records
  • Temperature-controlled distribution, qualified suppliers
Applies to
Pharma manufacturers and distributors
Assurance
Regulator inspection

Information security & privacy

ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Certifiable standard

Information security management system (ISMS).

  • Risk assessment and Statement of Applicability
  • Annex A: 93 controls in 4 themes: organisational (37), people (8), physical (14), technological (34)
  • Internal audit, management review, continual improvement
Applies to
Any platform handling business data, including a tax engine
Assurance
Certification audit; 3-year cycle

ISO/IEC 27017 · 27018

Code of practice

Cloud security controls (27017) and protection of personal data in public clouds (27018).

  • Shared responsibility
  • Customer data handling by cloud providers
Applies to
SaaS and cloud providers
Assurance
Usually assessed with 27001

ISO/IEC 27701

Certifiable standard

Privacy information management extending 27001.

  • Controller and processor controls
  • Maps to GDPR and similar laws
Applies to
Platforms processing personal data
Assurance
Certification audit

ISO/IEC 42001

Certifiable standard

AI management system (published 2023).

  • AI risk and impact assessment
  • Data governance, transparency, human oversight
  • Lifecycle controls for AI systems
Applies to
Teams using AI for IDP, forecasting, agents
Assurance
Certification audit

SOC 2

Attestation report

Controls against the AICPA Trust Services Criteria.

  • Security (always included), availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, privacy
  • Type I or Type II (usually 6–12 months)
  • Common criteria: control environment, risk, monitoring, logical access, change management, operations
Applies to
SaaS vendors, including procurement and tax platforms
Assurance
Independent CPA examination

SOC 3

Attestation report

Public summary of a SOC 2 examination.

  • General-use report without control details
Applies to
Vendors wanting a shareable report
Assurance
CPA examination

GDPR

Regulation (EU)

Protection of personal data of people in the EU.

  • Principles (Art. 5): lawfulness, fairness and transparency; purpose limitation; data minimisation; accuracy; storage limitation; integrity and confidentiality; accountability
  • Lawful basis, data subject rights, DPIAs, processor contracts, 72-hour breach notification, cross-border transfer safeguards
Applies to
Anyone processing EU personal data (supplier contacts, customers, employees)
Assurance
Data protection authorities

India DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025

Law (India)

Digital personal data protection; Rules notified November 2025 with phased implementation over 12–18 months.

  • Notice and consent, purpose limitation
  • Security safeguards and breach intimation
  • Data principal rights, grievance redressal
  • Additional duties for significant data fiduciaries
Applies to
Organisations processing personal data in India
Assurance
Data Protection Board of India

EU AI Act

Regulation (EU)

Risk-based rules for AI systems, applying in phases from 2025.

  • Prohibited practices, high-risk system obligations, transparency for generative AI
  • Documentation, human oversight, data quality
Applies to
Providers and deployers of AI in the EU
Assurance
Market surveillance authorities

OWASP ASVS · OpenAPI · OAuth 2.0 / OIDC

Technical standard

Application security verification, API description and authorisation standards.

  • Secure coding requirements
  • Token-based access, least privilege
Applies to
Engineering teams
Assurance
Testing and pen tests

Cyber & resilience frameworks

ISO 22301

Certifiable standard

Business continuity management system.

  • Business impact analysis
  • Continuity strategies and plans
  • Exercises and testing
Applies to
Critical suppliers, platform operators
Assurance
Certification audit

ISO 31000

Guidance

Risk management principles and process.

  • Identify, analyse, evaluate, treat, monitor risks
  • Integrate risk into governance
Applies to
All organisations
Assurance
Not certifiable

NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

Framework

Voluntary cyber risk framework (2024).

  • Six functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover
  • Profiles and tiers
  • Explicit supply chain risk management
Applies to
Any organisation
Assurance
Self-assessment or third-party

NIST SP 800-161 · SP 800-53

Framework

Cyber supply chain risk management (800-161) and the security control catalogue (800-53).

  • Supplier risk assessment, SBOMs, acquisition controls
  • Control families: access, audit, configuration, incident response and more
Applies to
Government suppliers, critical infrastructure
Assurance
Assessment

CIS Controls v8.1

Framework

18 prioritised security controls with implementation groups.

  • Asset and software inventory, data protection, secure configuration, access control, logging, service provider management
Applies to
Mid-size IT teams
Assurance
Self-assessment

EU NIS2 Directive

Regulation (EU)

Cybersecurity obligations for essential and important entities, including supply chain security.

  • Risk management measures, incident reporting (24h early warning)
  • Management accountability, supplier security
Applies to
Many sectors in the EU, including manufacturing, transport, digital providers
Assurance
National authorities

EU DORA

Regulation (EU)

Digital operational resilience for financial entities (applies since January 2025).

  • ICT risk management, incident reporting, resilience testing
  • Third-party ICT provider oversight
Applies to
Banks, insurers, payment firms and their ICT providers
Assurance
Supervisors

CERT-In Directions (India, 2022)

Regulation (India)

Cyber incident reporting and log retention.

  • Report specified incidents within 6 hours
  • Keep ICT logs for 180 days in India
  • Synchronise clocks to NTP sources
Applies to
Organisations operating in India
Assurance
CERT-In

Payments & finance

SOC 1 (SSAE 18 / ISAE 3402)

Attestation report

Controls at a service organisation relevant to customers' financial reporting.

  • Control objectives over transaction processing
  • Type I (design at a point in time) or Type II (operating effectiveness over a period)
Applies to
Tax engines, payroll, AP outsourcing, payment processors
Assurance
Independent CPA examination; report shared under NDA

PCI DSS v4.0.1

Industry standard

Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard for anyone storing, processing or transmitting cardholder data.

  • 1 Install and maintain network security controls
  • 2 Apply secure configurations
  • 3 Protect stored account data
  • 4 Protect cardholder data with strong cryptography in transit
  • 5 Protect systems from malicious software
  • 6 Develop and maintain secure systems and software
  • 7 Restrict access by business need to know
  • 8 Identify users and authenticate access
  • 9 Restrict physical access
  • 10 Log and monitor all access
  • 11 Test security regularly
  • 12 Support security with policies and programmes
  • Future-dated v4 requirements became mandatory on 31 March 2025
Applies to
Merchants, payment processors, platforms with card payments (e.g. p-cards, e-commerce checkout)
Assurance
Self-assessment questionnaire or QSA audit by merchant level; quarterly ASV scans

SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley) §404

Law (US)

Internal control over financial reporting for US-listed companies.

  • Management assessment and auditor attestation of ICFR
  • IT general controls: access, change management, operations, backup
  • Applies to tax, AP and revenue systems
Applies to
US-listed companies and their key systems
Assurance
External auditor

ISO 20022

Messaging standard

Financial messaging (payments, statements, cash management).

  • pain.001 payment initiation, pain.002 status, camt.053 statements
  • Structured remittance data
Applies to
Treasury, AP, banks
Assurance
Bank conformance testing

IFRS 15 / Ind AS 115 / ASC 606 · IAS 2 / Ind AS 2

Accounting standard

Revenue recognition and inventory valuation.

  • Five-step revenue model
  • Inventory at lower of cost and net realisable value; no LIFO under IFRS
Applies to
Finance and ERP design
Assurance
External audit

Supply chain, trade & data

ISO 28000

Certifiable standard

Security and resilience management for the supply chain.

  • Security risk assessment across the chain
  • Controls for facilities, cargo and partners
  • Incident response
Applies to
Ports, logistics providers, shippers
Assurance
Certification audit

GS1 standards

Data standard

Identification and data sharing: GTIN, GLN, SSCC, GS1-128 and DataMatrix barcodes, EPCIS 2.0 event data, GDSN.

  • Unique identifiers with check digits
  • Event data: what, where, when, why
Applies to
Manufacturers, retailers, logistics
Assurance
GS1 membership and label compliance

ANSI X12 · UN/EDIFACT

Data standard

EDI message standards (850/ORDERS, 856/DESADV, 810/INVOIC and more).

  • Partner implementation guides
  • Acknowledgements (997/CONTRL)
Applies to
Trading partners
Assurance
Partner certification

Incoterms® 2020

ICC rules

Trade terms defining risk, cost and responsibilities.

  • 11 rules; risk transfer point and cost split
Applies to
Buyers, sellers, forwarders
Assurance
Contractual

Harmonized System (HS)

Classification

WCO product classification used for customs and, in India, as the basis of HSN codes for GST.

  • 6-digit international codes; national extensions (8-digit in India)
  • HS 2022 edition in force; periodic revisions
Applies to
Importers, exporters, tax engines
Assurance
Customs

WCO SAFE / AEO · C-TPAT

Programme

Authorised Economic Operator and US Customs-Trade Partnership Against Terrorism trusted-trader programmes.

  • Security of premises, cargo and partners
  • Faster customs clearance
Applies to
Importers, exporters, logistics
Assurance
Customs validation

ISO 3166 · ISO 4217 · ISO 8601 · ISO 6346

Data standard

Country codes, currency codes, date/time format, freight container codes.

  • Use them in every interface and tax determination
Applies to
All systems
Assurance
—

ISPM 15

Phytosanitary standard

Treatment and marking of wood packaging (pallets) in international trade.

  • Heat treatment or fumigation mark
Applies to
Exporters
Assurance
Border inspection

SCOR Digital Standard

Reference model

ASCM process and metrics reference for supply chains.

  • Orchestrate, Plan, Order, Source, Transform, Fulfill, Return
  • Standard metrics such as perfect order fulfilment
Applies to
Supply chain teams

Sustainability & ESG

ISO 20400

Guidance

Sustainable procurement.

  • Integrate sustainability into procurement policy, strategy and process
  • Supplier engagement
Applies to
Procurement teams
Assurance
Not certifiable

GHG Protocol · ISO 14064 · ISO 14083 · GLEC

Accounting standard

Greenhouse gas accounting (Scopes 1–3), verification, and transport emissions (ISO 14083, GLEC Framework).

  • Scope 3 categories including purchased goods and transport
  • Verification statements
Applies to
Companies reporting emissions
Assurance
Third-party verification

EU CSRD / ESRS · CSDDD · CBAM · EUDR

Regulation (EU)

Sustainability reporting, corporate due diligence, carbon border adjustment and deforestation-free products; scope and dates have been revised several times.

  • Double materiality reporting (CSRD)
  • Human-rights and environmental due diligence (CSDDD)
  • Embedded emissions for imports such as steel and aluminium (CBAM definitive period from 2026)
  • Traceability to plot of land for covered commodities (EUDR)
Applies to
Companies in or selling into the EU
Assurance
National authorities; verify current applicability dates

SA8000 · RBA Code · SMETA · OECD Due Diligence Guidance

Social standard

Labour and human-rights standards and audits for suppliers.

  • No forced or child labour, health and safety, wages and hours, grievance mechanisms
Applies to
Suppliers and brands
Assurance
Social audits

India BRSR

Regulation (India)

SEBI Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting for top listed companies, with value-chain disclosures being phased in.

  • ESG disclosures and assurance for BRSR Core
Applies to
Listed companies and key value-chain partners
Assurance
SEBI

E-invoicing & tax

EN 16931 · Peppol · UBL · CII

Data standard

European e-invoice semantic model and common syntaxes and network.

  • Core invoice data model
  • Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 over the Peppol network
Applies to
Anyone invoicing in the EU
Assurance
Format validation

India e-invoice (INV-01 schema, IRP)

Regulation (India)

GST e-invoice JSON schema reported to the Invoice Registration Portal.

  • IRN and signed QR
  • 30-day reporting limit for larger taxpayers
Applies to
Businesses above the turnover threshold
Assurance
GSTN validation

OECD SAF-T

Data standard

Standard Audit File for Tax: export of accounting and tax data for audits.

  • Master files, general ledger, sales and purchase invoices, payments
Applies to
Countries requiring it (for example Portugal, Poland, Norway, Romania)
Assurance
Tax authority submissions

Which standards apply to which system

SystemStandards
Tax engine and invoice platformSOC 1 Type II (financial reporting), SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, SOX ITGC (if customers are US-listed), country e-invoicing schemas (IRP, Peppol/EN 16931, KSeF, SDI), SAF-T, record retention rules
Payment and card flowsPCI DSS (tokenise to stay out of scope), ISO 20022 bank files, SOC 1
Personal data (supplier contacts, customers)GDPR, India DPDP, ISO 27701, data residency where required
AI and IDP componentsISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act obligations where applicable, model and prompt change control, evaluation evidence
Warehouses and plantsISO 9001, ISO 45001, ISO 14001, sector standards (GMP/GDP, ISO 22000, IATF 16949)
Logistics and tradeIncoterms 2020, HS classification, AEO / C-TPAT, ISO 28000, ISPM 15, GS1 SSCC labels
Supplier onboardingISO 37001 anti-bribery due diligence, SA8000 / RBA / SMETA social audits, CSDDD and forced-labour rules, cyber questionnaires (NIST 800-161)
Sustainability reportingGHG Protocol, ISO 14083 / GLEC for freight, CSRD/ESRS, BRSR, CBAM data for covered imports
Operations and resilienceISO 22301, NIST CSF 2.0, NIS2 / DORA where applicable, CERT-In 6-hour reporting in India
